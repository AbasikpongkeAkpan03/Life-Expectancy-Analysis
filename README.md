# Life-Expectancy-Analysis
This involves the step-by-step Analysis of Life Expectancy Dataset.


### Project Overview
This project aims to analyse and extract key insights into life expectancies in various countries and regions or Continents over time. By analysing this dataset, trends and patterns are discovered, life-supporting recommendations and a real understanding of the data provided.

### Result and Findings
The results are summarized as follows:
- Europe leads with the highest contribution (32.31%) to total life expectancy, followed by Americas (23.05%) and Africa (21.44%). Oceania and Asia have smaller contributions at 8.00% and 24.20%, respectively.
- Europe with the highest life expectancy because of its prominent and well equiped medical facilities to help in human body treatment.
- Women consistently outlive men across all regions. The widest gender gap is in Europe, while the smallest is in Africa.
- Iceland, Japan, and Sweden top the list with the highest life expectancy. Niger, Nigeria, and Chad record the lowest life expectancy.
- A steady increase in average life expectancy over the years is observed.

### Recommendations
Based on the findings, I strongly recommend the following:
- More attention should be paid to the medical sector in countries.
- Good medical facilities should be provided.
- Good technologies should be provided to keep records of the life expectancy of countries as some of the countries had no data.

### Limitations
There are countries with no data in it which make little or no inconsistency in the analysis.
